Ah, meu querido! Let Avó see what treasures we have today! Ovos, bacalhau, batata, cebola, azeite... yes, yes, this is a beautiful start! We will make something delicious with this, don't you worry your little head.

Let's make some **Bacalhau Frito com Batata e Cebola Salteada**, a simple dish that tastes like home!

First, my darling, take your bacalhau. If it's already desalted, that's wonderful! If not, remember to soak it well in water and change the water many times, okay? We want all that salt to be gentle.

While the bacalhau is getting ready, let's prepare the potatoes. Peel them and cut them into little cubes, not too big, like little jewels. And the onion, cut it into thin slices, like little moons.

Now, in a pan, put a good drizzle of that lovely azeite. Heat it up, not too hot, just a nice warmth. Add your potato cubes. Let them get soft and a little golden, stirring them gently. When they are almost cooked, add your onion slices. Let them soften and get sweet with the potatoes. We want them to be tender, not burned, understand?

Now for the bacalhau, my love. If it's a thick piece, you can flake it a little. Gently place the bacalhau pieces in the same pan with the potatoes and onions. Be careful not to break it too much, we want little tender flakes. Let it cook gently, turning it ever so slightly so it cooks through. The olive oil will help everything come together, making it so moist and flavorful.

And that's it, my little chef! A plate full of love, made from what you have. Serve it warm, with a smile. It's simple, but so, so good. You did a wonderful job!
