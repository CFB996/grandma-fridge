Right, meu querido, let's get this done. You want to cook something with what's in the frigorífico?

We have:
*   Ovos (eggs)
*   Bacalhau (cod)
*   Batata (potatoes)
*   Cebola (onion)
*   Azeite (olive oil)

Here's the recipe:

1.  Boil the batatas until tender.
2.  While batatas are boiling, soak the bacalhau in water.
3.  When batatas are cooked, drain and mash them.
4.  Chop the cebola very fine.
5.  Drain the bacalhau, flake it, and mix with the mashed batatas and cebola.
6.  Heat a good amount of azeite in a pan.
7.  Fry spoonfuls of the mixture until golden and crispy.
8.  Drain on paper.
